# Resources
Additional voice training sources that will supplement the exercises.

## Spectrogram
All of the exercises, most notably the spoken sentences, can be preformed into a Spectrogram for a more quantifiable feedback. See [HOW TO USE A SPECTROGRAM | Voice Feminization by TransVoiceLessons](https://youtu.be/z4hio1fIDNk?si=cpBqTnrupaRR3qyq) for more information on how to read a Spectrogram in the context of voice training.

### Spectrogram Software
- [Friture](https://friture.org/)

## Recording Software
You may, optionally, use a program like [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) to record voice samples after doing some exercises. This can be an extremely emotionally taxing part of practice, only do so if you feel prepared to hear yourself. Also be aware that low quality microphones may have a masculinizing effect on your voice by attenuating higher frequencies.
